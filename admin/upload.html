How to use

Attaching an image to a product

  1. Upload the image here and copy the returned /uploads/… path.
  2. Open Products, edit the piece, and paste the path into Image path.
  3. Or skip this page entirely and use the Or upload a new image field inside the product form — it posts the file with the product in one request.
